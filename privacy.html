Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Content Automation handles information when users access and use the application.

2. Information We Process

When a user connects a TikTok account, AI Content Automation may process basic account information made available through TikTok's official APIs and the permissions granted by the user.

The application may also process content required to provide its features, including images, audio, videos, captions, and other content submitted or generated by the user.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide application functionality, including:

  • Authenticating connected accounts.
  • Generating and processing digital content.
  • Uploading content to supported platforms.
  • Publishing content when explicitly authorized by the user.
  • Maintaining and improving application functionality.

4. TikTok Integration

AI Content Automation uses official TikTok APIs to access TikTok functionality. Access is limited to permissions authorized by the user.

Users may revoke the application's TikTok permissions through TikTok's account settings where supported.

5. Third-Party Services

AI Content Automation may use third-party services for automation, artificial intelligence, media processing, storage, and content publishing. Information necessary to perform these functions may be processed by those services according to their respective privacy policies.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by the application. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only as necessary to provide the application's functionality, comply with applicable requirements, and maintain the service.

8. User Choices

Users may stop using the application or revoke access to connected third-party accounts at any time.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the application and its integrations change. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy can be submitted through the project owner.
